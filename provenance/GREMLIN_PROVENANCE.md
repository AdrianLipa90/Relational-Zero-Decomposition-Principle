# GREMLIN Provenance

GREMLIN is credited in this project as a proof-search, dependency-mapping, route-reduction, adversarial-audit, and provenance system.

Its role is deliberately separated from theorem authority.

GREMLIN may:
- construct cross-repository dependency maps;
- search candidate proof routes;
- identify missing bridges and circular dependencies;
- generate counterexamples to over-strong intermediate claims;
- minimize proof paths;
- compile provenance receipts.

GREMLIN does **not** automatically promote a candidate route to a theorem. Final promotion requires independent verification against pinned mathematical sources, formal kernels where available, and explicit claim-ledger gates.

This separation follows the provenance discipline established in the RH worked case.
