\begin{abstract}
We introduce the Relational-Zero Decomposition Principle (RZDP), a structural classification mapping distinct Millennium-problem obligations to different mathematical properties of a distinguished relational zero. The construction does not identify the problems as equivalent. Instead, it separates location, multiplicity, spectral isolation, algebraic realization, dynamical avoidance, and computational separation into typed proof obligations. The Riemann Hypothesis provides the first worked case, conditional on the Universal Relational-Zero Axiom A0. All other branches retain explicit theorem-status firewalls.
\end{abstract}

\section{Relational-Zero Principle}

Let a structure admit a canonical relation \(R\) and a non-negative faithful defect
\[
D_R\ge 0,\qquad D_R=0\Longleftrightarrow Z_R.
\]

\begin{principle}[Universal Relational-Zero Axiom A0]
A zero of an admitted observable is realized only as vanishing relational distinction in its defining canonical relation.
\end{principle}

The role of A0 is explicit. A theorem derived from it remains conditional on A0 unless A0 is independently established in the ambient standard theory.

\section{Relational-Zero Decomposition Principle}

For a problem \(P\) with distinguished relational zero \(Z_R\), define a typed obligation
\[
P\longmapsto\mathcal F_P(Z_R).
\]

The working decomposition is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{RH} &\longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Location}(Z_R),\\
\mathrm{BSD} &\longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Multiplicity/Index}(Z_R),\\
\mathrm{Yang\!-\!Mills} &\longleftrightarrow \operatorname{SpectralIsolation}(Z_R),\\
\mathrm{Hodge} &\longleftrightarrow \operatorname{AlgebraicRealization}(Z_R),\\
\mathrm{Navier\!-\!Stokes} &\longleftrightarrow \operatorname{DynamicalAvoidance}(Z_R),\\
P\;vs\;NP &\longleftrightarrow \operatorname{ResourceSeparation}(Z_R).
\end{aligned}
\]

\begin{remark}
RZDP is a decomposition principle, not a claim that the six problems are mutually equivalent.
\end{remark}

\section{Riemann Hypothesis: Location of Zero}

The RH branch is the worked reference case. Three complementary certificates target the same locus \(\Re s=1/2\): an algebraic fixed-point defect, a Shannon/KL information defect with derived coercivity, and a reciprocal-conjugation/projective defect.

The exact logical status carried into this repository is
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{A0}\Longrightarrow \mathrm{RH}}.
\]

\section{Multiplicity--Index Transfer}

Let \(A(z):\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^n\) be a holomorphic matrix family near \(z=0\). Put
\[
K=\ker A(0),\qquad C=\operatorname{coker}A(0),\qquad r=\dim K=\dim C.
\]
Let \(q:\mathbb C^n\to C\) be the quotient map and define
\[
\Gamma_A=q\circ A'(0)|_K:K\to C.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Relational Multiplicity--Index Transfer and Excess]
Assume \(\det A\) is not identically zero. Then
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{ord}_{z=0}\det A(z)\ge \dim\ker A(0).
}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{ord}_{z=0}\det A(z)=\dim\ker A(0)
\iff
\Gamma_A\text{ is an isomorphism}.
}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose a domain complement \(X\) and a codomain complement \(\widetilde C\) such that \(\mathbb C^n=K\oplus X\), \(\mathbb C^n=\widetilde C\oplus Y\), \(Y=\operatorname{im}A(0)\), and \(\widetilde C\cong C\) under the quotient map. In block form,
\[
A(z)=\begin{pmatrix}a(z)&b(z)\\c(z)&d(z)\end{pmatrix},
\]
where \(a(0)=b(0)=c(0)=0\) and \(d(0):X\to Y\) is invertible. Thus, for small \(z\),
\[
\det A(z)=\det d(z)\det\!\bigl(a(z)-b(z)d(z)^{-1}c(z)\bigr).
\]
Since \(b(z)d(z)^{-1}c(z)=O(z^2)\),
\[
a(z)-b(z)d(z)^{-1}c(z)
=
z\bigl(\Gamma_A+O(z)\bigr).
\]
Every entry of this \(r\times r\) Schur complement is divisible by \(z\), so its determinant is divisible by \(z^r\). Hence
\[
\operatorname{ord}_{z=0}\det A(z)\ge r.
\]
The coefficient of \(z^r\) is \(\det d(0)\det\Gamma_A\), which is nonzero exactly when \(\Gamma_A\) is invertible. This proves both statements.
\end{proof}

Define the multiplicity excess
\[
\varepsilon(A):=
\operatorname{ord}_{z=0}\det A(z)-\dim\ker A(0).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\varepsilon(A)\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0},}
\qquad
\boxed{\varepsilon(A)=0\iff\Gamma_A\text{ is an isomorphism}.}
\]
This theorem is independent of BSD. It gives a precise nonnegative measure of failure of transverse zero-mode crossing.

\section{Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer: Multiplicity and Index}

For an elliptic curve \(E/\mathbb Q\), write
\[
r_{\rm an}(E)=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s),
\qquad
r_{\rm MW}(E)=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}E(\mathbb Q).
\]
The Clay rank statement is \(r_{\rm an}(E)=r_{\rm MW}(E)\) \cite{WilesBSD2000}.

Set \(L_E(z)=L(E,1+z)\). If \(r=r_{\rm an}(E)\), then locally
\[
L_E(z)=z^r u(z),\qquad u(0)\ne0.
\]
Hence, for sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
\boxed{
r_{\rm an}(E)
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{|z|=\varepsilon}\frac{L'_E(z)}{L_E(z)}\,dz
=
\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb C\{z\}}
\frac{\mathbb C\{z\}}{(L_E)}.
}
\]
Thus analytic rank is simultaneously a winding number and a local-algebra length.

On the arithmetic side let
\[
V_E=E(\mathbb Q)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q.
\]
By Mordell--Weil,
\[
\boxed{
r_{\rm MW}(E)
=
\dim_{\mathbb Q}V_E
=
\max\{k:\Lambda^kV_E\ne0\}.
}
\]

Therefore the BSD rank statement is exactly the equality of two independently defined integer indices:
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb C\{z\}}
\frac{\mathbb C\{z\}}{(L_E)}
=
\max\{k:\Lambda^k(E(\mathbb Q)\otimes\mathbb Q)\ne0\}.
}
\]

A known theorem closes the parity shadow of this bridge: for every prime \(p\), the analytic rank and the \(p^\infty\)-Selmer rank have the same parity over \(\mathbb Q\) \cite{DokchitserDokchitser2010}. The full integer lift remains open.

The Selmer-complex formalism makes the Tate--Shafarevich correction explicit \cite{Nekovar2006}. Write
\[
r_p(E)=\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb Z_p}\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb Q),
\qquad
s_p(E)=\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb Z_p}\Sha(E/\mathbb Q)[p^\infty].
\]
The standard Selmer exact sequence gives
\[
r_p(E)=r_{\rm MW}(E)+s_p(E).
\]
By the \(p\)-parity theorem, \(r_{\rm an}-r_p\) is even. Hence
\[
k_p(E):=\frac{r_{\rm an}(E)-r_p(E)}{2}\in\mathbb Z
\]
and the BSD rank defect decomposes exactly as
\[
\boxed{
r_{\rm an}(E)-r_{\rm MW}(E)=2k_p(E)+s_p(E).
}
\]
No sign is asserted for \(k_p\), so this identity does not separately force the two terms to vanish.

The existing project-side unilateral-shift Fredholm theorem supplies an exact integer-index model, but no canonical BSD comparison map to that operator has yet been proved. It therefore cannot be used as the missing lift.

The current RZDP-BSD obligation is to construct a canonical arithmetic--analytic index object \(\mathcal I_E\) and prove independently
\[
\mathcal I_E
=
\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb C\{z\}}
\frac{\mathbb C\{z\}}{(L_E)}
\]
and
\[
\mathcal I_E
=
\dim_{\mathbb Q}(E(\mathbb Q)\otimes\mathbb Q).
\]
Only then does the BSD branch promote to Q.E.D.

Equivalently, the Multiplicity--Index Transfer theorem yields a three-gate operator criterion. It is sufficient to construct \(A_E(z)\) such that
\[
\det A_E(z)=u_E(z)L(E,1+z),\qquad u_E(0)\ne0,
\]
\[
\ker A_E(0)\cong E(\mathbb Q)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb C,
\]
and the induced crossing map \(\Gamma_{A_E}\) is invertible. These are labelled B1, B2 and B3 respectively. Their conjunction implies BSD rank equality, but none may be defined using that equality.

The determinant-line formulation of the Tamagawa-number conjecture shows that determinants of perfect Galois-cohomology complexes are the standard arithmetic setting in which such analytic/arithmetic comparisons live \cite{NguyenDet2015}. This identifies a canonical search space, not a completed proof.

There is also an exact local cohomological realization of the crossing map. Let \(R=K[[T]]\) and suppose a perfect amplitude-\([1,2]\), Euler-characteristic-zero complex is represented locally by
\[
R^n\xrightarrow{A(T)}R^n.
\]
After specialization at \(T=0\),
\[
H^1=\ker A(0),\qquad H^2=\operatorname{coker}A(0).
\]
The Bockstein map induced by the augmentation \(R\to K\) is, up to the conventional generator/sign of \((T)/(T^2)\),
\[
\boxed{\beta=q\circ A'(0)|_{\ker A(0)}.}
\]
Hence nondegenerate Bockstein implies
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\det A(T)=\dim_KH^1.}
\]
In self-dual Selmer-complex settings the Bockstein is the mechanism underlying \(p\)-adic height/regulator constructions; modern leading-term theories make this derivative structure explicit \cite{BurnsKuriharaSano2025,BenoisBuyukboduk2026}. Thus B3 is not an unspecified transversality condition: arithmetically it is a regulator-nondegeneracy gate. This still leaves the global analytic registration, main-conjecture/determinant comparison, specialization/control, and \(\Sha\)-divisible gates open.

More generally, without assuming nondegeneracy,
\[
\boxed{
\varepsilon(C_R)
:=
\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}\det A(T)-\dim_KH^1(C_0)
\ge0,
}
\]
and
\[
\varepsilon(C_R)=0
\iff
\beta\text{ is an isomorphism}.
\]
Thus, once the global registration identifies the determinant order with \(r_{\rm an}(E)\) and the specialized \(H^1\)-dimension with \(r_p(E)\), one obtains
\[
\boxed{
\varepsilon_{E,p}=r_{\rm an}(E)-r_p(E)\ge0.
}
\]
By parity, \(\varepsilon_{E,p}=2k_p(E)\) is then an even nonnegative integer.

The natural common carrier is the determinant line
\[
\Delta_{E,p}=\operatorname{Det}^{-1}_R C_{E,p}.
\]
For any invertible rank-one \(R\)-module \(\Delta\) and nonzero meromorphic sections \(s_1=q\,s_2\),
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{ord}_I(s_1)-\operatorname{ord}_I(s_2)=\operatorname{ord}_I(q).
}
\]
Hence equality of multiplicities is equivalent to the relative trivialization ratio being a unit. Recent work proves, under mild hypotheses, that the determinant of a Nekov\'a\v{r} Selmer complex is canonically isomorphic to the corresponding Stark-system module \cite{MaciasCastilloSano2026}. This validates the determinant line as a canonical arithmetic carrier, but not the universal analytic registration needed for BSD.

Combining the registered multiplicity excess with the Selmer--\(\Sha\) exact sequence gives the sharpened conditional normal form
\[
\boxed{
r_{\rm an}(E)-r_{\rm MW}(E)
=
\varepsilon_{E,p}+s_p(E),
\qquad
\varepsilon_{E,p}\ge0,\quad s_p(E)\ge0.
}
\]
Therefore, under the global registration hypotheses,
\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{BSD}_{\rm rank}
\iff
\varepsilon_{E,p}=0
\ \text{and}\
s_p(E)=0.
}
\]
The local theorem supplies nonnegativity; the unresolved content is the universal analytic/determinant registration, specialization/control, regulator nondegeneracy, and \(\Sha\)-divisible control.

\section{Yang--Mills: Spectral Isolation of Zero}

A unique zero mode does not imply a positive mass gap. The target is therefore quantitative, for example a coercive spectral estimate
\[
H\ge m(I-P_0),\qquad m>0,
\]
together with the continuum and reconstruction hypotheses required by the standard problem.

The continuum mass-gap bridge remains OPEN until a scale-stable positive physical gap is rigorously transferred.

\section{Hodge Conjecture: Algebraic Realization of Zero}

For codimension \(p\), define
\[
Q^p(X)=
\frac{H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)}
{\operatorname{im}(CH^p(X)\otimes\mathbb Q\to H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q))}.
\]

The conjectural target is \(Q^p(X)=0\). RZDP therefore types Hodge as an algebraic-realization problem. Defining a defect using the desired algebraic-cycle image does not itself prove the realization theorem.

\section{Navier--Stokes: Dynamical Avoidance}

A generic barrier template is
\[
D^+B(t)\ge-c(t)B(t),\qquad B(0)>0,\qquad \int_0^T c(t)\,dt<\infty,
\]
which implies
\[
B(t)\ge B(0)\exp\!\left(-\int_0^t c(\tau)\,d\tau\right)>0.
\]

The open obligation is to bind a legitimate Navier--Stokes control quantity \(B\) to such a barrier without assuming the desired regularity.

\section{P versus NP: Computational Separation}

A successful route requires a complexity invariant \(\Phi\) compatible with composition and polynomial resources, while forcing super-polynomial resource growth for an NP-complete family. Any candidate must be audited against established lower-bound barriers.

\section{Synthesis}

The common object is not a universal solution formula but a typed zero obligation:
\[
Z_R\mapsto
\{\text{location},\text{multiplicity},\text{gap},\text{realization},\text{avoidance},\text{resource separation}\}.
\]

A branch moves from OPEN\_BRIDGE or REDUCTION to THEOREM only after every problem-specific bridge is independently proved and provenance-checked.
